## 1. Problem & research findings

**Course topic (fixed):** Topic 6, Life Cycle Cost Analysis (LCCA) Decision Tool. See `CM 617 - Course Project Topic.pdf`.

**Tool promise:** *Reach your IGBC target at the lowest life-cycle cost, with tariff risk measured.*

| # | Real Indian problem | Evidence | How the tool addresses it |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Choices are made on first cost (L1 tendering). Green buildings cost 2–17% more upfront, and that perception is the main barrier, yet life-cycle savings exceed 20% of construction cost | [Research Square study](https://researchsquare.com/article/rs-1498374/v1), [TERI](https://teriin.org/project/life-cycle-cost-energy-efficient-buildings) | Compares the min-capex and min-LCC portfolios side by side |
| P2 | Tariffs keep rising and rules keep changing. MERC set the rooftop-solar export credit at ₹2.82/kWh for FY25-26, and multi-year tariff orders cut C&I solar savings | [SolarQuarter – MERC tariff](https://solarquarter.com/2025/04/09/maharashtra-electricity-regulatory-commission-sets-rooftop-solar-tariff-at-%e2%82%b92-82-kwh-for-fy-2025-26/) | Monte Carlo on tariff escalation, plus break-even |
| P3 | Cooling demand is booming. Buildings use ~33% of India's electricity (heading to 55% by 2047), and building cooling demand is set to grow ~11× from 2018 to 2038 (ICAP) | [PMC study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9360156), [IEA](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-efficiency-2023/can-efficient-cooling-help-manage-fast-rising-electricity-demand-in-india-and-achieve-thermal-comfort-for-all) | HVAC alternatives compared on LCC |
| P4 | Unequal lives and replacements are ignored. Simple payback misses inverter and battery replacements | Standard LCCA practice | Replacement cycles plus residual value, with EAC |
| P5 | Data entry is manual. Vendor quotes and BOQs arrive as PDFs and get retyped | Team observation | AI reads the quote PDF and fills in the inputs |
| P6 | Green ratings need a cost case. ECBC 2017 was built on LCC analysis. IGBC credits (energy, renewables) earn incentives such as extra FAR | [UNDP ECBC rollout](https://files.acquia.undp.org/public/migration/in/Rolling-out-ECBC-Codes.pdf), [IGBC incentives](https://igbc.in/governmentincentivestoigbc) | IGBC credit optimizer, plus FAR benefit valuation |

**IGBC notes**
- IGBC Green New Buildings **v4.0** is mandatory for new registrations from 1 May 2026. EE Credit 2 (Enhanced Energy Performance) is worth up to 13 points and is simulation-based. ([SimulationHub](https://www.simulationhub.com/blog/building-analysis-in-igbc))
- In v3.0, on-site renewables earned 2/4/6 points for >1/3/5% of annual energy. ([IGBC v3 addendum](https://www.igbc.in/frontend-assets/html_pdfs/GBC_Green_New_Buildings_v3.0-Addendum_(Technical)_Dec_2014.pdf))
- Maharashtra reportedly gives +3/5/7% FAR for Silver, Gold and Platinum. This comes from a secondary source and is **not verified**. ([Realtynmore](https://realtynmore.com/maharashtra-and-mumbai-pioneering-sustainable-built-environment-leadership-in-india/))

## 7. Demo script & checklist
1. Load the Pune office demo: base points 52, target Gold (70)
2. Measures: upload `SunRise_Solar_100kWp_quote.pdf`, review the AI-extracted fields, add to library
3. LCCA: split AC vs VRF vs chiller. VRF has the higher capex but the lowest EAC
4. IGBC Optimizer: min-capex Gold vs min-LCC Gold, then toggle on the FAR benefit
5. Risk: the discount sweep's rank flip, break-even, Monte Carlo P(min-LCC wins)
6. Report: AI owner summary, then download Excel
